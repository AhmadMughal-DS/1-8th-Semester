## Word by Word Explanation (Roman Urdu Mein)

### Bonjour (بونژوغ)

**Bolne ka tareeqa:** "bon-zhoor"  
**Roman Urdu mein:** Bon-zhooغ  
**Yaad rakhne ka tareeqa:** Sabse formal aur common greeting! Subah se shaam tak istemal hota hai.  
**Context:** Office, stores, strangers, elders  
**Misaal:** "Bonjour, Monsieur" (Hello, Sir)

---

### Bonsoir (بون سواغ)

**Bolne ka tareeqa:** "bon-swahr"  
**Roman Urdu mein:** Bon-swaغ  
**Yaad rakhne ka tareeqa:** Shaam ko formal greeting.  
**Context:** Evening meetings, dinner events  
**Misaal:** "Bonsoir, Madame" (Good evening, Madam)

---

### Comment allez-vous? (کو ماں تالے وو)

**Bolne ka tareeqa:** "koh-mahn tah-lay voo"  
**Roman Urdu mein:** Koh-mahn tah-lay voo  
**Yaad rakhne ka tareeqa:** "Aap kaise hain?" - Bohot formal!  
**Context:** Professional settings, elderly people, authorities  
**Response:** "Je vais bien, merci" (Main theek hoon, shukriya)  
**Misaal:** "Bonjour! Comment allez-vous?" (Hello! How are you?)

---

### Enchanté / Enchantée (آں شاں تے)

**Bolne ka tareeqa:** "ahn-shahn-tay"  
**Roman Urdu mein:** Ahn-shahn-tay  
**Yaad rakhne ka tareeqa:** "Aapse milkar khushi hui" - Pehli mulaqat par!  
**Gender difference:**  
- Enchanté (masculine - larka bole)
- Enchantée (feminine - larki bole)  
**Context:** Business meetings, formal introductions  
**Misaal:** "Je suis Pierre. Enchanté!" (Main Pierre hoon. Khushi hui!)

---

### Au revoir (او رُہ ووار)

**Bolne ka tareeqa:** "oh ruh-vwahr"  
**Roman Urdu mein:** Oh ruh-vwahr  
**Yaad rakhne ka tareeqa:** Standard formal goodbye.  
**Context:** Leaving office, ending formal meetings  
**Misaal:** "Au revoir, Monsieur" (Goodbye, Sir)

---

### À bientôt (آ بیاں تو)

**Bolne ka tareeqa:** "ah byahn-toh"  
**Roman Urdu mein:** Ah byahn-toh  
**Yaad rakhne ka tareeqa:** "Jaldi milenge" - Formal lekin friendly.  
**Context:** When you'll meet again soon  
**Misaal:** "À bientôt!" (See you soon!)

---

### Bonne journée (بون ژورنے)

**Bolne ka tareeqa:** "bun zhoor-nay"  
**Roman Urdu mein:** Bun zhoor-nay  
**Yaad rakhne ka tareeqa:** "Aapka din achha rahe" - Very polite!  
**Context:** Leaving a shop, office, formal setting  
**Misaal:** "Merci! Bonne journée!" (Thank you! Have a good day!)

---

### Bonne soirée (بون سوارے)

**Bolne ka tareeqa:** "bun swah-ray"  
**Roman Urdu mein:** Bun swah-ray  
**Yaad rakhne ka tareeqa:** "Aapki shaam achhi rahe".  
**Context:** Leaving in the evening  
**Misaal:** "Au revoir! Bonne soirée!" (Goodbye! Have a good evening!)

---

### Bienvenue (بیاں وُہ نو)

**Bolne ka tareeqa:** "byahn-vuh-noo"  
**Roman Urdu mein:** Byahn-vuh-noo  
**Yaad rakhne ka tareeqa:** "Khush aamdeed" - Formal welcome.  
**Context:** Welcoming guests, clients, visitors  
**Misaal:** "Bienvenue à Paris!" (Welcome to Paris!)

---

### Allô (آلو)

**Bolne ka tareeqa:** "ah-loh"  
**Roman Urdu mein:** Ah-loh  
**Yaad rakhne ka tareeqa:** "Hello" - Sirf phone par!  
**Context:** Answering phone calls  
**Misaal:** "Allô? Qui est à l'appareil?" (Hello? Who's speaking?)

## Formal Conversation Example (Rasmee Guftugu)

### Meeting Someone First Time:

**Person A:** Bonjour, Monsieur. Je suis Marie Dupont.  
(Hello, Sir. I am Marie Dupont.)

**Person B:** Bonjour, Madame. Enchanté. Je m'appelle Jean Martin.  
(Hello, Madam. Pleased to meet you. My name is Jean Martin.)

**Person A:** Comment allez-vous?  
(How are you?)

**Person B:** Je vais très bien, merci. Et vous?  
(I'm very well, thank you. And you?)

**Person A:** Très bien, merci.  
(Very well, thank you.)

---

### Leaving Formally:

**Person A:** Je dois partir maintenant.  
(I must leave now.)

**Person B:** D'accord. Au revoir et bonne journée!  
(Alright. Goodbye and have a good day!)

**Person A:** Merci. Au revoir. À bientôt!  
(Thank you. Goodbye. See you soon!)

## Important Titles (Qaabil-e-Izzat Unwan)

### Use with Formal Greetings:

| **Title** | **Pronunciation** | **Meaning** | **Usage** |
|-----------|------------------|-------------|-----------|
| Monsieur | muh-syuh | Sir/Mr. | Adult men |
| Madame | mah-dahm | Madam/Mrs. | Adult/married women |
| Mademoiselle | mahd-mwah-zel | Miss | Young unmarried women (less common now) |
| Docteur | dok-tur | Doctor | Medical doctors |
| Professeur | proh-feh-sur | Professor | Teachers/professors |

### Examples:

- "Bonjour, **Monsieur** Dupont" (Hello, Mr. Dupont)
- "Bonsoir, **Madame** Martin" (Good evening, Mrs. Martin)
- "Au revoir, **Docteur**" (Goodbye, Doctor)

## When to Use Formal Greetings? (Kab Istemal Karein?)

### ✅ Use Formal Greetings When:

1. **At work/office** (Kaam ki jagah)
2. **With strangers** (Anjaan logon ke saath)
3. **With elderly people** (Buzurgon ke saath)
4. **In shops/restaurants** (Dukanon/restaurants mein)
5. **With teachers/professors** (Ustaadon ke saath)
6. **With bosses/authorities** (Afsar ke saath)
7. **In government offices** (Sarkari daftaron mein)
8. **First meetings** (Pehli mulaqat)
9. **Professional emails** (Professional emails mein)
10. **Formal events** (Rasmee taqreebat mein)

## Formal Greeting Rules (Rasmee Qawaneen)

### Rule 1: Always Use "Vous"
Use **"vous"** (formal you) instead of **"tu"** (informal you)

❌ Wrong: "Comment vas-tu?" (Too casual)  
✅ Right: "Comment allez-vous?" (Formal)

### Rule 2: Add Titles
Always add Monsieur/Madame when greeting formally

❌ Wrong: "Bonjour!" (Too casual with strangers)  
✅ Right: "Bonjour, Monsieur!" (Respectful)

### Rule 3: Full Phrases
Use complete greetings, not shortened versions

❌ Wrong: "Salut!" (Informal)  
✅ Right: "Bonjour!" (Formal)

### Rule 4: Proper Goodbye
Use formal goodbye phrases when leaving

❌ Wrong: "Ciao!" or "Bye!" (Too casual)  
✅ Right: "Au revoir!" or "À bientôt!" (Formal)

## Time-Based Formal Greetings (Waqt Ke Mutabiq)

### Morning (Subah - 6 AM to 12 PM):
- **Bonjour** ✅

### Afternoon (Dopahar - 12 PM to 6 PM):
- **Bonjour** ✅ (still used!)

### Evening (Shaam - 6 PM to 10 PM):
- **Bonsoir** ✅

### Night (Raat - 10 PM onwards):
- **Bonne nuit** ✅ (when leaving/sleeping)

---

## Quick Summary (Khulasa)

**Most Important Formal Greetings:**

1. **Bonjour** - Default formal greeting (subah to shaam)
2. **Bonsoir** - Evening formal greeting
3. **Comment allez-vous?** - Formal "how are you?"
4. **Enchanté(e)** - Nice to meet you (first time)
5. **Au revoir** - Formal goodbye
6. **Merci** - Thank you (always polite)

**Golden Rule:**  
When in doubt, use **"Bonjour, Monsieur/Madame"** - yeh hamesha safe hai! ✅
