## Introduction (Taaruf)

Informal greetings wo hain jo hum **doston, family, aur apne umar ke logon** ke saath bolte hain. Yeh casual aur friendly hote hain.

## Word by Word Explanation (Roman Urdu Mein)

### Salut (سالو)

**Bolne ka tareeqa:** "sah-loo"  
**Roman Urdu mein:** Sah-loo  
**Yaad rakhne ka tareeqa:** "Hi/Hello" - Doston ke saath!  
**Context:** Friends, family, people your age  
**Misaal:** "Salut! Ça va?" (Hi! How are you?)  
**Note:** NEVER use with elders or strangers!

---

### Ça va? (سا وا)

**Bolne ka tareeqa:** "sah vah"  
**Roman Urdu mein:** Sah vah  
**Yaad rakhne ka tareeqa:** "Kaise ho?" - Super casual!  
**Context:** Asking friends how they are  
**Response:** "Ça va bien!" (I'm good!) or just "Ça va!" (Good!)  
**Misaal:** "Salut Pierre! Ça va?" (Hi Pierre! How are you?)

---

### Coucou (کو کو)

**Bolne ka tareeqa:** "koo-koo"  
**Roman Urdu mein:** Koo-koo  
**Yaad rakhne ka tareeqa:** "Hey!" - Bohot cute aur friendly!  
**Context:** Close friends, children, playful greeting  
**Misaal:** "Coucou! Tu es là?" (Hey! Are you there?)  
**Note:** Used mostly by/with close friends or children

---

### Quoi de neuf? (کوا دہ نف)

**Bolne ka tareeqa:** "kwah duh nuf"  
**Roman Urdu mein:** Kwah duh nuf  
**Yaad rakhne ka tareeqa:** "Kya naya hai?" / "What's up?"  
**Context:** Casual conversation starter  
**Response:** "Rien de spécial" (Nothing special) or "Pas grand-chose" (Not much)  
**Misaal:** "Salut! Quoi de neuf?" (Hi! What's new?)

---

### À tout à l'heure (آ تو تا لُر)

**Bolne ka tareeqa:** "ah too tah lur"  
**Roman Urdu mein:** Ah too tah lur  
**Yaad rakhne ka tareeqa:** "Thodi der mein milenge" - Same day!  
**Context:** When you'll see them later the same day  
**Misaal:** "Je pars. À tout à l'heure!" (I'm leaving. See you later!)  
**Short form:** "À tout!" or "À toute!" (very casual)

---

### À demain (آ دہ ماں)

**Bolne ka tareeqa:** "ah duh-mahn"  
**Roman Urdu mein:** Ah duh-mahn  
**Yaad rakhne ka tareeqa:** "Kal milenge".  
**Context:** Saying goodbye, will meet tomorrow  
**Misaal:** "Bonne nuit! À demain!" (Good night! See you tomorrow!)

---

### Ciao (چاؤ)

**Bolne ka tareeqa:** "chow"  
**Roman Urdu mein:** Chow  
**Yaad rakhne ka tareeqa:** "Bye" - Italian word, bohot casual!  
**Context:** Very casual goodbye with friends  
**Misaal:** "Ciao! À plus!" (Bye! See ya!)  
**Note:** Some people use "Tchao" (French spelling)

---

### Bisous (بی زو)

**Bolne ka tareeqa:** "bee-zoo"  
**Roman Urdu mein:** Bee-zoo  
**Yaad rakhne ka tareeqa:** "Kisses/Hugs" - Bohot friendly!  
**Context:** Close friends, family (especially in messages)  
**Misaal:** "À demain! Bisous!" (See you tomorrow! Hugs!)  
**Short form:** "Biz" (in texts)

---

### À plus (آ پلوس)

**Bolne ka tareeqa:** "ah ploos"  
**Roman Urdu mein:** Ah ploos  
**Yaad rakhne ka tareeqa:** "Later!" - Very casual.  
**Context:** Casual goodbye, "see ya later"  
**Full form:** "À plus tard" (see you later)  
**Misaal:** "Je dois y aller. À plus!" (I gotta go. Later!)  
**Short form:** "A+" (in texts)

---

### Prends soin de toi (پراں سواں دہ توا)

**Bolne ka tareeqa:** "prahn swahn duh twah"  
**Roman Urdu mein:** Prahn swahn duh twah  
**Yaad rakhne ka tareeqa:** "Apna khayal rakhna" - Caring!  
**Context:** When someone is leaving for long or facing difficulty  
**Misaal:** "Au revoir! Prends soin de toi!" (Goodbye! Take care!)

---

## Informal Conversation Example (Ghair Rasmee Guftugu)

### Meeting a Friend:

**Friend A:** Salut! Ça va?  
(Hi! How are you?)

**Friend B:** Ça va bien! Et toi?  
(I'm good! And you?)

**Friend A:** Super! Quoi de neuf?  
(Great! What's new?)

**Friend B:** Rien de spécial. On va au café?  
(Nothing special. Wanna go to a café?)

**Friend A:** Oui, bonne idée!  
(Yes, good idea!)

---

### Saying Goodbye to a Friend:

**Friend A:** Bon, je dois partir maintenant.  
(Well, I have to leave now.)

**Friend B:** D'accord! À tout à l'heure?  
(Okay! See you later?)

**Friend A:** Non, demain. À demain!  
(No, tomorrow. See you tomorrow!)

**Friend B:** OK! Bisous!  
(OK! Hugs!)

**Friend A:** Ciao!  
(Bye!)

## Informal vs Formal - Quick Comparison

| **Situation** | **Informal** | **Formal** |
|--------------|-------------|-----------|
| Greeting | Salut | Bonjour |
| How are you? | Ça va? | Comment allez-vous? |
| You (singular) | Tu | Vous |
| Goodbye | Ciao / À plus | Au revoir |
| See you later | À tout! | À bientôt |
| Nice to meet you | Enchanté (casual) | Enchanté, Monsieur/Madame |

## When to Use Informal Greetings? (Kab Istemal Karein?)

### ✅ Use Informal Greetings When:

1. **With close friends** (Qarubi doston ke saath)
2. **With family members** (Khandan ke saath)
3. **With classmates** (Class fellows ke saath)
4. **With people your age** (Apni umar ke logon ke saath)
5. **In casual settings** (Ghair rasmee mahaul mein)
6. **At parties** (Parties mein)
7. **With children** (Bachchon ke saath)
8. **In text messages** (Messages mein)
9. **After someone says "Use tu"** (Jab koi kahe "tu bolo")

### ❌ DON'T Use Informal With:

1. **Strangers** (Anjaan log)
2. **Elderly people** (Buzurg)
3. **Teachers/Professors** (Ustaad)
4. **Bosses** (Afsar)
5. **In shops** (Dukanon mein)
6. **Professional settings** (Professional jagahon par)
7. **First meetings** (Pehli mulaqat)

## Informal Greeting Rules (Ghair Rasmee Qawaneen)

### Rule 1: Use "Tu" (Not "Vous")
Use **"tu"** (informal you) with friends

✅ Right: "Comment vas-**tu**?" (How are you? - casual)  
❌ Wrong: "Comment allez-**vous**?" (Too formal for friends)

### Rule 2: Drop Titles
No need for Monsieur/Madame with friends

✅ Right: "Salut Marie!" (Hi Marie!)  
❌ Wrong: "Salut Madame Marie!" (Too formal)

### Rule 3: Shortened Forms OK
You can use short versions

✅ "À tout!" instead of "À tout à l'heure!"  
✅ "À plus!" instead of "À plus tard!"  
✅ "Ciao!" instead of "Au revoir!"

### Rule 4: More Expressive
You can be more emotional and playful

✅ "Coucou!" (Cute greeting)  
✅ "Bisous!" (Hugs/kisses)  
✅ "Salut mon ami!" (Hi my friend!)

## French Cheek Kisses (La Bise)

### What is it?
Physical greeting with cheek kisses (air kisses)

### When?
- Meeting friends/family
- Informal social gatherings
- Not for formal settings!

### How many kisses?
- **Paris:** 2 kisses (left cheek first)
- **Southern France:** 3 kisses
- **Some regions:** 4 kisses!

### With whom?
- ✅ Friends
- ✅ Family
- ✅ Close colleagues (informal)
- ❌ Strangers
- ❌ Professional settings
- ❌ First meetings

## Quick Summary (Khulasa)

**Most Important Informal Greetings:**

1. **Salut** - Hi/Hello (friends)
2. **Ça va?** - How are you? (casual)
3. **Coucou** - Hey! (playful)
4. **Ciao** - Bye (very casual)
5. **À demain** - See you tomorrow
6. **Bisous** - Hugs/kisses (goodbye)

**Golden Rule:**  
Only use informal greetings when you know the person well or they tell you to use "tu"! 👥
